The Healthcare TOFU is Dead: Why Local Entity Trust Now Trumps National Authority

For years, healthcare marketers used top-of-funnel (TOFU) content as a brand discovery engine. Data now shows that AI has turned this strategy into a "Domain Authority Tax" with zero direct ROI.

Introduction

There is a fundamental shift happening in healthcare search that marketers have struggled to quantify. For a decade, the playbook was simple: publish hundreds of articles on "knee pain causes" or "signs of osteoarthritis" to capture patients at the Top of the Funnel (TOFU). The assumption was that a patient reading your blog would remember your brand when they eventually needed surgery.

Our latest investigation reveals this assumption was always a myth—and in the age of AI Overviews (AIO), it has become a liability. Patients do not read TOFU content to pick a hospital; they read it for neutral medical answers. Today, AI search engines are providing those answers directly on the SERP, stripping away the traffic while leaving healthcare systems to pay the "content tax" just to maintain their domain authority.

Key Takeaways

  • 89% AIO Coverage: Healthcare informational queries now trigger AI Overviews at the highest rate of any industry, effectively terminating the user journey on the search page (BrightEdge).
  • 65% CTR Collapse: Click-through rates for informational health queries have plummeted by over 60% since mid-2024 as AI answers satisfy intent without a click (Omnibound).
  • The "Rising Tide" Paradox: TOFU content no longer drives brand discovery; it functions solely as a Domain Authority signal to lift Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU) rankings.
  • Local Entity Dominance: For procedures like knee replacements, AI search isolates brand discovery to the BOFU, where local entity trust and proximity override national authority.

The Data Behind this Study

This report synthesizes search volatility data from 2023–2025, specifically focusing on the healthcare sector. We analyzed data from BrightEdge's Generative Parser, which tracks over 10,000 healthcare keywords, and Omnibound's CTR impact studies. The methodology involved comparing the performance of "Informational" (TOFU) queries against "Transactional/Local" (BOFU) queries in the wake of Google's AI Overview rollout.

Definitions

  • AI Overview (AIO): The generative summary at the top of Google search results that synthesizes multiple sources into a single answer.
  • The Rising Tide Effect: The SEO theory that high-volume informational content builds sitewide domain authority, which in turn lifts the rankings of high-value service pages.
  • Zero-Click CTR Degradation: The phenomenon where search engines satisfy user intent on the SERP, leading to a collapse in referral traffic to source websites.
  • Entity Trust: The degree of confidence a search engine has in a specific, physical business entity (e.g., a local surgical center) based on verified data like NPI, reviews, and location signals.

Findings: The Great Funnel Compression

The data shows a clear bifurcation in how healthcare search operates. While TOFU queries are being cannibalized by AI, BOFU queries remain resilient because they require physical, local execution that an LLM cannot provide.

The "Knee Replacement" Test Case

Consider the journey for a knee replacement. When a patient searches for "exercises for knee pain," they are in a neutral educational state. AI Overviews now provide these exercises directly. The patient gets what they need without ever seeing the hospital's logo or visiting their site.

However, the moment the query shifts to "Robotic-assisted knee replacement surgeons near me," the search engine switches from a generative mode to a retrieval mode. It pulls from the Knowledge Graph—focusing on surgical volume, patient reviews, and geographic proximity. National media publishers (WebMD, Healthline) are stripped away, and local entity trust becomes the only currency that matters.

What this means for your brand

The traditional SEO approach for TOFU has fundamentally changed. If your strategy relies on "blogging for traffic," you are fighting a losing battle against the AI summary. Here is the new strategic framework for healthcare operators:

  1. Accept the "Authority Tax": Continue publishing high-quality TOFU content, but stop measuring it by "clicks." Its only role now is to maintain the Rising Tide that keeps your BOFU pages ranking.
  2. Pivot to Entity SEO: Focus on the signals AI cannot synthesize: your surgeons' specific outcomes, your facility's unique technology (e.g., Mako robotics), and your verified patient reviews.
  3. Own the BOFU: Invest heavily in procedure-specific pages that answer "How do you do it?" and "Why are you the best local choice?" rather than "What is it?"
  4. Leverage Owned Channels: Use your TOFU content in email and paid social where you still control the brand experience. In search, TOFU is now for the machines; BOFU is for the patients.
